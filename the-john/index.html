Marigny

The John

Two blocks off Frenchmen and a world away from it, The John is where the Marigny drinks when it doesn't feel like being a tourist. The toilet-themed decor is a running joke that became an identity. The drinks are cheap and heavy-handed. The jukebox is good. There is a ping-pong table and a pool table and, reviewers keep noting, a genuinely clean bathroom.

No kitchen, and that's by design: order from any of the spots on Frenchmen or St. Claude and bring it in. Cash only, ATM inside, no attitude.

Questions, answered

What are the hours at The John?

The John is open Monday to Saturday 10am to 3am; Sunday 10am to 12am. Hours come from its Google listing, so check the live status at the top of this page before you head over.

Where is The John?

The John is at 2040 Burgundy St, New Orleans, LA 70116 in Marigny, New Orleans. Burgundy St at Frenchmen, Marigny

Is The John cash only?

Yes. The John is listed as cash only, so bring cash.

Does The John take reservations?

No. The John doesn't take reservations, so just walk in.

Is there parking at The John?

There's free street parking near The John.

Is The John wheelchair accessible?

The John lists a wheelchair accessible restroom.

When is The John least busy?

Google's visit data shows it busiest around 10pm on Saturdays. One of the quietest open times is 10am on Thursdays.

What is The John known for?

Regulars come for the The Well Drink, Ping Pong, The Jukebox and the Bring Your Own Food. Reviews keep mentioning strong drinks, cash only, jukebox, friendly bartender, ping pong table.
