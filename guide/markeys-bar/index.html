Markey's Bar

Bar in Bywater, New Orleans. Markey's is where the neighborhood watches the Saints, drinks a real pint, and argues about shuffleboard. Crawfish boils in season, a big beer list, and a corner that has been a bar for generations.

4.5★ from 486 Google reviews · 640 Louisa St, New Orleans, LA 70117

Directions  @markeysbar on Instagram

Markey's sits on the corner of Louisa and Royal in the Bywater, in a building that has been pouring drinks since long before the neighborhood got interesting. The black-and-white photo behind the bar of the old crew under the 7-Up sign tells you how far back it goes. The locals inside tell you it never really changed.

It is a sports bar in the old sense: a wall of screens for the Saints and the Premier League, a serious beer list that runs from Miller High Life to the local IPAs, a Guinness that gets poured properly, darts, and the shuffleboard table that settles arguments. In crawfish season the boils spill onto the sidewalk tables.

What to order at Markey's Bar

  • Crawfish Boil: Spring weekends, by the tray, with corn and potatoes. Watch Instagram for boil days.
  • The Guinness: Poured in two parts and let to settle, the way the regulars insist on.
  • Shuffleboard & Darts: The long table in the back decides who buys the next round.
  • Game Day: Every screen, every Saints game, and the Premier League early on Saturdays.

Local tip

Bike over on the Crescent Park path and lock up out front. Kickoff Sundays fill by noon; get there for the pregame.
