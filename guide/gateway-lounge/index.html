Gateway Lounge is a bar at 200 Bourbon St in the French Quarter. 37 people have reviewed it on Google, and the same things keep coming up: tacos, nachos, walking, queso.

Regulars describe it as casual and a little hip. It works for groups. One reviewer put it simply: "The lounge itself is welcoming and has a local, relaxed feel, making it a great place to stop by, especially if you’re in the area and looking to take a break from walking around the city."
