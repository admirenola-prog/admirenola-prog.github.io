Beach On Bourbon

Bar in French Quarter, New Orleans. The Beach on Bourbon is the party, seven nights a week. Live music and DJs, drunk trivia on Tuesdays, Saints and college football on the big screens, and a courtyard when you need air.

4.4★ from 3,117 Google reviews · 227 Bourbon St, New Orleans, LA 70130

Directions

Two blocks off Canal, The Beach is the room Bourbon Street built for a crowd. A stage that runs live bands and DJs into the small hours, a dance floor that fills by ten, a wall of screens for game day, and a courtyard out back with its own bar and a fountain, which is where you go when you need five minutes and a breeze.

It is where bachelorette parties, conference groups, and Saints fans end up on the same night and somehow get along. Afrobeats nights, Who Dat takeovers, trivia with free shots for right answers. Bartenders like Natasha get name-checked in reviews. Bring the group; there is room.

What to order at Beach On Bourbon

  • The Hurricane: Bourbon Street's strongest, in the souvenir cup. One is a good idea. Two is a story.
  • Live Music: Bands and DJs nightly. Cover bands early, DJs late, Afrobeats and Amapiano nights on the calendar.
  • The Courtyard: Open air, its own bar, a fountain, and a screen. The quiet part of the loudest bar on the street.
  • Game Day: Saints, LSU, Tulane, and whoever you flew in for, on every screen with the sound up.

Local tip

Book VIP seating by text for a big group, then send the half that needs a breather to the courtyard. Everyone stays in the same building.
