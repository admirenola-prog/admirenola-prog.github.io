Chez Pierre Bakery Lakeview

Bakery in West End, New Orleans. Lakeview's little French bakery with a Vietnamese kitchen in back. Breakfast, lunch, pastries, and cakes for every occasion on Harrison Avenue.

4.3★ from 63 Google reviews · 141 Harrison Ave, New Orleans, LA 70124

Directions  @chezpierrefrenchbakery on Instagram

Chez Pierre is the Lakeview bakery that does two things at once and does both properly. The front case is French: croissants, eclairs, fruit tarts, Eiffel Tower chocolates, king cake in season, and layer cakes decorated to order for birthdays, weddings, and Saints Sundays. The frozen matcha has a following.

The kitchen is Vietnamese, the way the best New Orleans bakeries often are. Banh mi on bread baked that morning, pho, summer rolls, crawfish pies, meat pies. Reviewers say people are sleeping on this place. Lindsay behind the counter would like that to stop.

What to order at Chez Pierre Bakery Lakeview

  • Breakfast Croissant: Sausage, egg, cheese on a house croissant. Reviewers use the word bomb.
  • Banh Mi: Grilled pork or chicken, pickled carrot, cilantro, pate, on bread from the oven behind you.
  • Custom Cakes: Birthdays, weddings, Mardi Gras. Order a week out, pick up on Harrison.
  • King Cake: Carnival season only. Traditional, filled, and the purple-green-gold done right.

Local tip

Order the cake by phone, then pick up a banh mi and a frozen matcha when you come to get it. Nobody leaves with just the cake.
