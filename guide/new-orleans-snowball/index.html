New Orleans Snowball

Dessert shop in Gentilly Terrace, New Orleans. New Orleans Snowball on Elysian Fields does the classic right: shaved-fine ice, house syrups, stuffed with cheesecake or ice cream if you want, plus hot dogs and po'boys for the ride home.

4.5★ from 627 Google reviews · 4313 Elysian Fields Ave, New Orleans, LA 70122

Directions

Ask anyone in Gentilly where they get a snowball and you'll hear this corner. The ice is shaved fluffy, not crunchy, the syrup list runs long enough that regulars have a standing order, and the condensed-milk drizzle is the move the reviews keep mentioning. Cheesecake-stuffed is the order for the people who know.

It is more than a dessert window. Hot dogs, sausage po'boys, nachos, and sugar-free flavors for the folks who still want in. A patio out front, a line on hot afternoons, and staff that keep it moving with a smile.

What to order at New Orleans Snowball

  • Double Cherry, Condensed Milk: The one people write reviews about. Add a chunk of cheesecake.
  • Stuffed Snowball: Vanilla ice cream or cheesecake in the middle. Two desserts, one cup.
  • Nachos & Po'boys: Nachos with cheese, hot dogs, sausage po'boys from the grill in back.
  • Sugar-Free Flavors: A real list, not an afterthought.

Local tip

Bring cash. The line moves fastest that way, and the small is plenty.
