Bertha's Place

Bar in South 7th Ward, New Orleans. Bertha's is the 7th Ward's party. Live jazz and R&B some nights, a DJ others, a kitchen behind the bar doing pork chop and chicken plates, and an owner who walks the room.

4.4★ from 673 Google reviews · 1355 St Bernard Ave, New Orleans, LA 70116

Directions  @popular on Instagram

Bertha's Place is the kind of neighborhood club New Orleans used to be full of and now protects. St. Bernard Avenue, a corner bar with a stage, a dance floor that fills after eleven, and a crowd that dresses for it. Grown and sexy, as one reviewer put it. Birthday parties book the back.

The surprise is the kitchen. Pork chop plates, chicken plates, potato salad, at prices that make the second round easy. Bertha herself introduces herself to musicians who wander over after their own gigs across the street. That is the whole spirit of the place.

What to order at Bertha's Place

  • The Live Band: Jazz, R&B, and a singer who works the room. Check the door for the week's nights.
  • Pork Chop Plate: Smothered, with rice and potato salad, from the counter behind the bar.
  • The Dance Floor: Fills after eleven. Grown folks, good music, no drama.
  • Birthday Parties: Bring the crew, book a section, the DJ will call your name.

Local tip

Go on a live-band night and get there before ten to get a table. The band starts around eleven and the floor is gone by midnight.
