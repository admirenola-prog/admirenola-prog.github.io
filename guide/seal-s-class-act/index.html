Seal's Class Act

Bar in Seventh Ward, New Orleans. Seal's Class Act is an adults-only neighborhood club in the 7th Ward: live bands, a dance floor, food trucks out front, and an owner the regulars call auntie.

4.4★ from 399 Google reviews · 2169 Aubry St, New Orleans, LA 70119

Directions

Seal's is the 7th Ward's living room after dark. Ms. Seal has run it long enough that reviewers call her family. Live bands most weekends, a DJ between sets, a dance floor, bar and table seating, and a small cover at the door that keeps the room grown.

There is no kitchen, so the food trucks post up outside and the plates come in. It is a real neighborhood club: friendly crowd, good music, nobody in a hurry to leave.

What to order at Seal's Class Act

  • The Live Band: Weekends. R&B, blues, brass. Check the door for who's on.
  • Ms. Seal: Greets you with a smile, remembers you next time. The reviews say it, we're repeating it.
  • The Food Trucks: Out front on band nights. Bring the plate in.
  • The Dance Floor: Grown folks only. Dress like you mean it.

Local tip

Get there before the band starts for a table. Cover at the door, one-drink minimum, then the night is yours.
