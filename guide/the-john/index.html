The John

Bar in Marigny, New Orleans. The John is exactly what it sounds like: a neighborhood bar on Burgundy with cheap strong drinks, a jukebox, ping pong, and bartenders who remember you. Bring food, bring cash.

4.3★ from 571 Google reviews · 2040 Burgundy St, New Orleans, LA 70116

Directions  @thejohnneworleans on Instagram

Two blocks off Frenchmen and a world away from it, The John is where the Marigny drinks when it doesn't feel like being a tourist. The toilet-themed decor is a running joke that became an identity. The drinks are cheap and heavy-handed. The jukebox is good. There is a ping-pong table and a pool table and, reviewers keep noting, a genuinely clean bathroom.

No kitchen, and that's by design: order from any of the spots on Frenchmen or St. Claude and bring it in. Cash only, ATM inside, no attitude.

What to order at The John

  • The Well Drink: Strong, cheap, and the reason the reviews say 'heavy pours'.
  • Ping Pong: One table, house paddles, winner stays.
  • The Jukebox: Actually good. Locals fight over it.
  • Bring Your Own Food: Frenchmen is two blocks away. Grab it, bring it, eat at the bar.

Local tip

Late on a weeknight is the sweet spot. The Frenchmen crowd thins out and the ping-pong table opens up.
