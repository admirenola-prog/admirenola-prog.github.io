Beach On Bourbon

4.4★ · 3,117 Google reviews

Two blocks off Canal, The Beach is the room Bourbon Street built for a crowd. A stage that runs live bands and DJs into the small hours, a dance floor that fills by ten, a wall of screens for game day, and a courtyard out back with its own bar and a fountain, which is where you go when you need five minutes and a breeze.

Pub

Cajun Mikes Pub & Grub

4.3★ · 1,091 Google reviews

Cajun Mike's has been on Baronne Street since 2000, which in this part of the CBD makes it an elder. It is a bar first: sixteen-ish stools, a long wood top, TVs for the game, and bartenders who refill before you ask. Regulars call it a hole in the wall and mean it as the highest compliment.

Order: Hot Roast Beef Po'boy, Cochon de Lait, Boudin Balls

Local tip: Two blocks from the Roosevelt and the Saenger. Perfect pre-show, and it is where the bartenders from the fancy places drink after.

Vietnamese restaurant

Viet Orleans Bistro

4.3★ · 852 Google reviews

Viet Orleans opened on the corner of Baronne and Gravier to feed the office towers and hotels around it, and it still does that better than anywhere in the CBD: a bowl of pho lands in about six minutes at the lunch rush. The broth is clear and long-simmered, the herbs come piled high, and the brisket is the cut regulars ask for.

Order: Seafood Pho, Volcano Roll, Grilled Pork Vermicelli

Local tip: Happy hour is the play: half the sushi menu, cold sake, and the room to yourself before the hotel crowd finds it.

Donut shop

4.2★ · 459 Google reviews

Bakery

4.3★ · 531 Google reviews

Norma's Sweets started as a Latin bakery and grew into the neighborhood's kitchen. The case up front holds tres leches, empanadas, guava-and-cheese pastries, and pan dulce.
