Ryan's Irish Pub Inc

Irish pub in French Quarter, New Orleans. Bring your food in, grab a stool, and settle in. This is the Quarter's corner pub for people who actually like pubs.

4.5★ from 875 Google reviews · 241 Decatur St, New Orleans, LA 70130

Directions  @places on Instagram

Ryan's sits on the corner of Decatur and Iberville, three blocks from Jackson Square and a world away from the daiquiri machines. Sixteen beers on tap, a wall of bottles, a full bar, and a proper pour of Guinness. The floor is old tile, the bar is old wood, and the bathrooms are, reviewers keep noting with some surprise, clean.

There is no kitchen, and that is the point. Order from anywhere in the Quarter and bring it in. The TVs carry the NFL, NHL, and MLB packages plus soccer, golf, racing, and whatever else is on. On a Saturday in the fall it is loud in the best way.

What to order at Ryan's Irish Pub Inc

  • Sixteen Taps: Local Urban South and NOLA Brewing next to Guinness, Harp, and Smithwick's.
  • The Guinness Pour: Two-part pour, settled properly. Reviewers call it the place to get a proper pint.
  • Irish Car Bombs: Five dollars. The sign has been up long enough to be part of the decor.
  • Game Day: Every screen, every package. Saints, Premier League, March Madness, the Masters.

Local tip

Grab a muffuletta from Central Grocery two blocks down, then come here for the pint. That is the local move.
