The Upper Quarter

Bar in French Quarter, New Orleans. The Upper Quarter is the small, friendly corner bar on Bienville where locals drink, visitors get adopted, and crawfish gets boiled on the sidewalk in spring.

4.7★ from 358 Google reviews · 1000 Bienville St, New Orleans, LA 70112

Directions  @theupperquarterbar on Instagram

The Upper Quarter calls itself the best neighborhood bar in the French Quarter, and the reviews are not arguing. It is small and mighty: a corner room at Bienville and Burgundy, bartenders named in nearly every review, a crowd that talks to strangers, and pudding shots for reasons that make sense after the second one.

Spring means crawfish boils. Fall means the Saints on every screen. Year round it means feeling like part of a community after two days, which is what visitors keep writing. After-work spot for the Quarter's service industry, which is the best endorsement a bar can carry.

What to order at The Upper Quarter

  • Crawfish Boils: Spring weekends on the sidewalk. Follow Facebook for boil days.
  • Pudding Shots: Yes. They're a thing here. Ask.
  • The Bartenders: Matthew, Riley, and the rest. Named in the reviews for a reason.
  • Game Day: Saints on every screen, the whole bar in black and gold.

Local tip

Sit at the corner of the bar and say hello. That's the entire strategy. You'll have five friends by the second drink.
