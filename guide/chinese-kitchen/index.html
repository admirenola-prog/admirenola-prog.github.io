Chinese Kitchen

Chinese restaurant in Gert Town, New Orleans. Classic Chinese takeout on S Carrollton. Egg rolls come up in more reviews than anything else on the menu.

4.1★ from 1,799 Google reviews · 3327 S Carrollton Ave, New Orleans, LA 70118

Directions

Chinese Kitchen is a plain takeout spot on S Carrollton, with a red sign out front and a parking lot you can pull into. Ask anyone who has ordered here and they'll bring up the egg rolls first. Reviewers mention them in 175 reviews, more than any other dish. Reviewers also name the shrimp fried rice and boneless chicken. The food comes in foam boxes, and a lot of it was homemade, one visitor from New York said.

Expect a wait at busy times. Long lines and wait time both come up often, and so does cash only, so bring cash. Portion size gets praised, and one visitor from New York called the prices reasonable. The reviews are split, though. Two reviewers said the fried rice came out hard, and one 1-star review called the food tasteless. The overall rating is 4.1 from 1,799 reviews.

What to order at Chinese Kitchen

  • Egg rolls: Mentioned in 175 reviews, more than any other dish on the menu.
  • Shrimp fried rice: Mentioned 31 times in reviews, second only to the egg rolls.
  • Boneless chicken: Mentioned 13 times. One reviewer found it fried too hard.
  • Beef lo mein: Noodles with beef, cabbage and green pepper in a brown sauce.

Local tip

Bring cash, phone ahead if you're in a hurry, and eat the fried food while it's hot. Two reviewers said the rice came out hard.
