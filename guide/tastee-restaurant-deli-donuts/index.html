Tastee Donuts & Deli

Donut shop in Seventh Ward, New Orleans. Tastee is the mom-and-pop donut shop New Orleans grew up on. Cake donuts, glazed, buttermilk drops, cinnamon rolls, and the tiny castle burgers that made the name, on Esplanade Avenue.

4.2★ from 459 Google reviews · 2549 Esplanade Ave, New Orleans, LA 70119

Directions

Tastee Donuts was once a citywide chain, and this Esplanade Avenue shop carries the name and the recipes into the present. The buttermilk drops are the reason people cross town. The apple fritters are the size of a plate. Cake donuts, glazed, jelly, cinnamon rolls, all fried in small batches.

The deli side does breakfast and the castle burgers, little sliders that people remember from childhood and order by the sack. It is a counter, a few tables, and cashiers the reviews call helpful and welcoming. Not fancy. Exactly right.

What to order at Tastee Donuts & Deli

  • Buttermilk Drops: The Tastee original. Dense, glazed, gone by ten.
  • Apple Fritter: Bigger than your hand, still warm if you time it.
  • Grits & Eggs Breakfast: Eggs, bacon, grits, toast from the deli side. Cheap and fast.
  • Cake Donuts: Plain, chocolate, sprinkled. The dozen for the office.

Local tip

Ask for whatever just came out. Buttermilk drops sell out first; grits and eggs are the sleeper breakfast.
