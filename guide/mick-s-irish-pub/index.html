Mick's Irish Pub

Bar in Mid-City, New Orleans. Mick's is Mid-City's game-room pub: fair prices, good bartenders, a quiet weekday vibe, and DaWeenies slinging hot dogs from the kitchen.

4.5★ from 375 Google reviews · 4801 Bienville Ave, New Orleans, LA 70119

Directions  @micksirishpub on Instagram

Mick's Irish Pub sits on the corner of Bienville and Bernadotte with a full game room: pool table, dart boards, pinball, ping pong, shuffleboard, video games. It is quiet on weekdays and lively on weekends and always fairly priced. The bartenders will make whatever you want as long as the bottle exists.

The kitchen is a pop-up, DaWeenies, doing what one reviewer called the best hot dog he has ever had. Outdoor seating out front, a jukebox inside, dogs welcome, tourists rare.

What to order at Mick's Irish Pub

  • DaWeenies Hot Dogs: The pop-up kitchen. Loaded dogs, hand-cut fries. Check the hours.
  • Pool & Darts: Two boards, one table, league nights.
  • Pinball: A row of machines that actually work.
  • Shuffleboard: The long table. Winner buys.

Local tip

Tuesday darts, Thursday pinball. Come on a weeknight if you want the tables to yourself.
