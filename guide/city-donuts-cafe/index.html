City Donuts & Café

Donut shop in Uptown/Carrollton, New Orleans. City Donuts is the 24-hour spot on Claiborne. Donuts and beignets made fresh through the night, a full breakfast and po'boy kitchen, and a drive-thru line that moves.

4★ from 1,034 Google reviews · 3301 S Claiborne Ave, New Orleans, LA 70125

Directions

Every neighborhood needs one place that is open when nothing else is. On South Claiborne it is City Donuts. Glazed, powdered, cake, blueberry, twists, and the beignets that reviewers drive across town for, fried in small batches all night long. The drive-thru is the reason half the city knows the place without ever having parked.

It is more than donuts. The kitchen runs a full breakfast and lunch: shrimp and grits, liver and grits, eggs and sausage, biscuits, po'boys, the after-the-bar plate that saves the next morning. Fast, friendly, cheap, and open. That is the whole pitch and it has worked for years.

What to order at City Donuts & Café

  • Glazed Beignets: Not the powdered-sugar kind. Fried, glazed, warm. The reason the drive-thru line exists.
  • Shrimp & Grits: A full plate of Gulf shrimp over cheese grits with peppers and onions. Breakfast that holds you till dinner.
  • The Donut Case: Glazed, chocolate, sprinkles, cake, twists, and the blueberry cake donut people ask for by name.
  • Breakfast Plate: Eggs, sausage or bacon, grits, toast. Under ten dollars and ready before you find your keys.

Local tip

Ask what just came out of the fryer. Whatever it is, get a dozen of that and let them box the rest.
