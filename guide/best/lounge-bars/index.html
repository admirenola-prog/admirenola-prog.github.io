Lounge bar · Bywater

BJ's Lounge

4.6★ from 388 Google reviews · 4301 Burgundy St

BJ's Lounge has been the Bywater's corner bar since before the Bywater was a destination. Cash only, cheap drinks, a jukebox, a courtyard out back, and Monday nights that belong to the blues. Little Freddie King, well into his eighties, still plays here. Reviewers say don't miss it. They are right.

Order: Little Freddie King, The Courtyard, The Jukebox
