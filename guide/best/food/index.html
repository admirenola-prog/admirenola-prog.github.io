Chinese restaurant · Gert Town

Chinese Kitchen

4.1★ from 1,799 Google reviews · 3327 S Carrollton Ave

Chinese Kitchen is a plain takeout spot on S Carrollton, with a red sign out front and a parking lot you can pull into. Ask anyone who has ordered here and they'll bring up the egg rolls first. Reviewers mention them in 175 reviews, more than any other dish. Reviewers also name the shrimp fried rice and boneless chicken. The food comes in foam boxes, and a lot of it was homemade, one visitor from New York said.

Order: Egg rolls, Shrimp fried rice, Boneless chicken

Donut shop · Uptown/Carrollton

City Donuts & Café

4★ from 1,034 Google reviews · 3301 S Claiborne Ave

Every neighborhood needs one place that is open when nothing else is. On South Claiborne it is City Donuts. Glazed, powdered, cake, blueberry, twists, and the beignets that reviewers drive across town for, fried in small batches all night long. The drive-thru is the reason half the city knows the place without ever having parked.

Order: Glazed Beignets, Shrimp & Grits, The Donut Case

Vietnamese restaurant · Central Business District

Viet Orleans Bistro

4.3★ from 852 Google reviews · 300 Baronne St

Viet Orleans opened on the corner of Baronne and Gravier to feed the office towers and hotels around it, and it still does that better than anywhere in the CBD: a bowl of pho lands in about six minutes at the lunch rush. The broth is clear and long-simmered, the herbs come piled high, and the brisket is the cut regulars ask for.

Order: Seafood Pho, Volcano Roll, Grilled Pork Vermicelli

Seafood restaurant · New Orleans East Area

Lucky Jean Seafood

4.2★ from 805 Google reviews · 6746 Chef Menteur Hwy

Lucky Jean's is the kind of place you find by asking someone from the East where they get their seafood. The answer comes fast. The little building on Chef Menteur Highway with the shrimp boat on the sign has been frying, boiling, and stuffing po'boys for the neighborhood for decades, and the regulars who have been coming since before the storm are still coming.

Order: Fried Shrimp, Shrimp Po'boy, Boiled Crawfish

Grocery store · Mid-City

Banks Meat Market

4.1★ from 731 Google reviews · 325 S Broad St

Banks Meat Market anchors the corner of Broad and Banks, and everyone within ten blocks has a Banks order. The butcher case in back runs deep: fresh cuts, smoked sausage by the pound, hot sausage patties, turkey necks, whatever the week's plate needs. The hot counter up front turns it into lunch.

Order: Hot Roast Beef Po'boy, The 32-Inch, Hot Sausage by the Pound

Brunch restaurant · Faubourg Lafayette

Cafe Porche & Snowbar

4.9★ from 644 Google reviews · 1625 Baronne St

Cafe Porche sits in a little white house on Baronne Street with tables out front under the trees. Mrs. Porche runs the room. The reviews say the same thing over and over: the food was amazing, and the people were kinder than they had to be. In a city with a lot of brunch, that combination is rarer than it sounds.

Order: Praline French Toast, Shrimp & Grits, Fried Catfish & Eggs

Dessert shop · Gentilly Terrace

New Orleans Snowball

4.5★ from 627 Google reviews · 4313 Elysian Fields Ave

Ask anyone in Gentilly where they get a snowball and you'll hear this corner. The ice is shaved fluffy, not crunchy, the syrup list runs long enough that regulars have a standing order, and the condensed-milk drizzle is the move the reviews keep mentioning. Cheesecake-stuffed is the order for the people who know.

Order: Double Cherry, Condensed Milk, Stuffed Snowball, Nachos & Po'boys

Bakery · Mid-City

Norma's Sweets Bakery

4.3★ from 531 Google reviews · 2925 Bienville St

Norma's Sweets started as a Latin bakery and grew into the neighborhood's kitchen. The case up front holds tres leches, empanadas, guava-and-cheese pastries, and pan dulce. The grill in back turns out Cuban sandwiches, baleadas, tamales, ribs, and a plate of the day. The shelves in between are a real Latin grocery: Nicaraguan coffee, produce, meat.

Order: Tres Leches, Cuban Sandwich, Baleadas

Donut shop · Seventh Ward

Tastee Donuts & Deli

4.2★ from 459 Google reviews · 2549 Esplanade Ave

Tastee Donuts was once a citywide chain, and this Esplanade Avenue shop carries the name and the recipes into the present. The buttermilk drops are the reason people cross town. The apple fritters are the size of a plate. Cake donuts, glazed, jelly, cinnamon rolls, all fried in small batches.

Order: Buttermilk Drops, Apple Fritter, Grits & Eggs Breakfast

Grocery store · French Quarter

Matassa's Market

4.4★ from 425 Google reviews · 1001 Dauphine St

Matassa's has been the neighborhood grocery at Dauphine and St. Philip for a century. Fresh fruit out front, a liquor and wine selection that surprises people, a deli counter that has fed the Quarter's bartenders and residents through every era. The recent renovation made it look new without making it feel new.

Order: Shawarma, Kofta Burger, The Deli

Seafood restaurant · Seventh Ward

Danny's Seafood

4.2★ from 403 Google reviews · 1812 Onzaga St

Danny's sits on a side street in the 7th Ward and feeds the neighborhood out of a counter window. Boiled shrimp and snow crab most days, crawfish when it's running, pig feet and turkey necks slow-cooked in the same spicy boil. The heat is real; ask for mild if you're not from here.

Order: Spicy Turkey Necks, Boiled Shrimp, Snow Crab

Chicken wings restaurant · St. Claude

Who Dat Wings & More

4.2★ from 373 Google reviews · 1611 Franklin Ave

Who Dat Wings & More is a counter on Franklin Avenue that does one thing seriously. Wings fried crisp, tossed to order in a long sauce list, and the fries that people plan around: cajun ranch, garlic parmesan, loaded with everything. Corn nuggets on the side because it's New Orleans.

Order: Sweet Chili Wings, Loaded Fries, Honey BBQ

Bakery · West End

Chez Pierre Bakery Lakeview

4.3★ from 63 Google reviews · 141 Harrison Ave

Chez Pierre is the Lakeview bakery that does two things at once and does both properly. The front case is French: croissants, eclairs, fruit tarts, Eiffel Tower chocolates, king cake in season, and layer cakes decorated to order for birthdays, weddings, and Saints Sundays. The frozen matcha has a following.

Order: Breakfast Croissant, Banh Mi, Custom Cakes
