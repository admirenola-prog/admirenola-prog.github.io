Cafe Porche & Snowbar

Brunch restaurant in Faubourg Lafayette, New Orleans. A family-run brunch spot in Central City with real coffee, a real patio, and the kind of service people write paragraphs about. Weekends mostly, so plan around it.

4.9★ from 644 Google reviews · 1625 Baronne St, New Orleans, LA 70113

Directions

Cafe Porche sits in a little white house on Baronne Street with tables out front under the trees. Mrs. Porche runs the room. The reviews say the same thing over and over: the food was amazing, and the people were kinder than they had to be. In a city with a lot of brunch, that combination is rarer than it sounds.

The plates are Southern and unhurried: French toast stacked with strawberries and bananas, shrimp and grits with a proper gravy, fried catfish with eggs, gumbo, red beans, a pork chop on the weekend. The coffee is taken seriously, the lattes get art, and the snowball window opens when it's hot, which is most of the year.

What to order at Cafe Porche & Snowbar

  • Praline French Toast: Thick-cut, custard-soaked, piled with berries and banana. The photo everyone posts.
  • Shrimp & Grits: Gulf shrimp, creamy grits, brown gravy. The savory order the regulars split with the French toast.
  • Fried Catfish & Eggs: Cornmeal-crusted catfish, two eggs, grits or fried grits. A Central City breakfast.
  • The Latte: Real espresso, real art. The pumpkin one comes back every fall and people wait for it.

Local tip

Come before 10:30 on Saturday or plan to wait on the porch, which honestly is not a bad way to spend twenty minutes.
