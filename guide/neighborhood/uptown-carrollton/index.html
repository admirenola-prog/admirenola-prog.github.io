City Donuts & Café

4★ from 1,034 Google reviews · 3301 S Claiborne Ave

Every neighborhood needs one place that is open when nothing else is. On South Claiborne it is City Donuts. Glazed, powdered, cake, blueberry, twists, and the beignets that reviewers drive across town for, fried in small batches all night long. The drive-thru is the reason half the city knows the place without ever having parked.
