Bar · French Quarter

Beach On Bourbon

4.4★ from 3,117 Google reviews · 227 Bourbon St

Two blocks off Canal, The Beach is the room Bourbon Street built for a crowd. A stage that runs live bands and DJs into the small hours, a dance floor that fills by ten, a wall of screens for game day, and a courtyard out back with its own bar and a fountain, which is where you go when you need five minutes and a breeze.

Order: The Hurricane, Live Music, The Courtyard

Irish pub · French Quarter

Ryan's Irish Pub Inc

4.5★ from 875 Google reviews · 241 Decatur St

Ryan's sits on the corner of Decatur and Iberville, three blocks from Jackson Square and a world away from the daiquiri machines. Sixteen beers on tap, a wall of bottles, a full bar, and a proper pour of Guinness. The floor is old tile, the bar is old wood, and the bathrooms are, reviewers keep noting with some surprise, clean.

Order: Sixteen Taps, The Guinness Pour, Irish Car Bombs

Grocery store · French Quarter

Matassa's Market

4.4★ from 425 Google reviews · 1001 Dauphine St

Matassa's has been the neighborhood grocery at Dauphine and St. Philip for a century. Fresh fruit out front, a liquor and wine selection that surprises people, a deli counter that has fed the Quarter's bartenders and residents through every era. The recent renovation made it look new without making it feel new.

Order: Shawarma, Kofta Burger, The Deli

Bar · French Quarter

Boondock Saint

4.7★ from 379 Google reviews · 731 St Peter

Named for the cult movie and run like a neighborhood pub, Boondock Saint is the French Quarter bar locals keep for themselves. It is small. It is dark in the good way. The Guinness pours right, the jukebox has taste, and the bartenders, Lacey among them, are the reason half the reviews exist.

Order: The Guinness, The Jukebox, The Bartenders

Bar · French Quarter

The Upper Quarter

4.7★ from 358 Google reviews · 1000 Bienville St

The Upper Quarter calls itself the best neighborhood bar in the French Quarter, and the reviews are not arguing. It is small and mighty: a corner room at Bienville and Burgundy, bartenders named in nearly every review, a crowd that talks to strangers, and pudding shots for reasons that make sense after the second one.

Order: Crawfish Boils, Pudding Shots, The Bartenders
