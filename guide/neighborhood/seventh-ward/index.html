Donut shop · Seventh Ward

Tastee Donuts & Deli

4.2★ from 459 Google reviews · 2549 Esplanade Ave

Tastee Donuts was once a citywide chain, and this Esplanade Avenue shop carries the name and the recipes into the present. The buttermilk drops are the reason people cross town. The apple fritters are the size of a plate. Cake donuts, glazed, jelly, cinnamon rolls, all fried in small batches.

Order: Buttermilk Drops, Apple Fritter, Grits & Eggs Breakfast

Seafood restaurant · Seventh Ward

Danny's Seafood

4.2★ from 403 Google reviews · 1812 Onzaga St

Danny's sits on a side street in the 7th Ward and feeds the neighborhood out of a counter window. Boiled shrimp and snow crab most days, crawfish when it's running, pig feet and turkey necks slow-cooked in the same spicy boil. The heat is real; ask for mild if you're not from here.

Order: Spicy Turkey Necks, Boiled Shrimp, Snow Crab

Bar · Seventh Ward

Seal's Class Act

4.4★ from 399 Google reviews · 2169 Aubry St

Seal's is the 7th Ward's living room after dark. Ms. Seal has run it long enough that reviewers call her family. Live bands most weekends, a DJ between sets, a dance floor, bar and table seating, and a small cover at the door that keeps the room grown.

Order: The Live Band, Ms. Seal, The Food Trucks
