Pub · Central Business District

Cajun Mikes Pub & Grub

4.3★ from 1,091 Google reviews · 116 Baronne St

Cajun Mike's has been on Baronne Street since 2000, which in this part of the CBD makes it an elder. It is a bar first: sixteen-ish stools, a long wood top, TVs for the game, and bartenders who refill before you ask. Regulars call it a hole in the wall and mean it as the highest compliment.

Order: Hot Roast Beef Po'boy, Cochon de Lait, Boudin Balls

Vietnamese restaurant · Central Business District

Viet Orleans Bistro

4.3★ from 852 Google reviews · 300 Baronne St

Viet Orleans opened on the corner of Baronne and Gravier to feed the office towers and hotels around it, and it still does that better than anywhere in the CBD: a bowl of pho lands in about six minutes at the lunch rush. The broth is clear and long-simmered, the herbs come piled high, and the brisket is the cut regulars ask for.

Order: Seafood Pho, Volcano Roll, Grilled Pork Vermicelli

Cigar shop · Central Business District

Don Leoncio Cigars Bar

4.4★ from 602 Google reviews · 430 Canal St

Don Leoncio is the lounge you hope exists when you walk off Bourbon Street wanting somewhere quieter. The neon says cigar bar, the room says gentleman's club without the pretension: deep leather couches, a long bar of whiskeys and rums, a humidor room glowing in the back, and a game on the screen.

Known for: Hand-Rolled Cigars, Cigar & Whiskey Pairing, The Couches

Bar · Central Business District

Little Bar on Gravier

4.8★ from 415 Google reviews · 510 Gravier St

Half a block from the big hotels on Gravier, a door you'd walk past. Inside: a short bar, a jukebox, a bartender who will know your name by the second drink, and pours that reviewers describe as heavy with a straight face. It is the bar the convention crowd finds by accident and comes back to on purpose.

Order: The Bloody Mary, The Sazerac, Heavy Pours
