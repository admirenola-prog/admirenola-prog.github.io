Cafe Porche & Snowbar

4.9★ from 644 Google reviews · 1625 Baronne St

Cafe Porche sits in a little white house on Baronne Street with tables out front under the trees. Mrs. Porche runs the room. The reviews say the same thing over and over: the food was amazing, and the people were kinder than they had to be. In a city with a lot of brunch, that combination is rarer than it sounds.

Order: Praline French Toast, Shrimp & Grits, Fried Catfish & Eggs
