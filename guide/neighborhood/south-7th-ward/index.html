Bertha's Place

4.4★ from 673 Google reviews · 1355 St Bernard Ave

Bertha's Place is the kind of neighborhood club New Orleans used to be full of and now protects. St. Bernard Avenue, a corner bar with a stage, a dance floor that fills after eleven, and a crowd that dresses for it. Grown and sexy, as one reviewer put it. Birthday parties book the back.
