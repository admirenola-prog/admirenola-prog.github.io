Chinese Kitchen

4.1★ from 1,799 Google reviews · 3327 S Carrollton Ave

Chinese Kitchen is a plain takeout spot on S Carrollton, with a red sign out front and a parking lot you can pull into. Ask anyone who has ordered here and they'll bring up the egg rolls first. Reviewers mention them in 175 reviews, more than any other dish. Reviewers also name the shrimp fried rice and boneless chicken. The food comes in foam boxes, and a lot of it was homemade, one visitor from New York said.

Order: Egg rolls, Shrimp fried rice, Boneless chicken
