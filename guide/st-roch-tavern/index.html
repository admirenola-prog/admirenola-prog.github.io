St Roch Tavern

Bar in St. Roch, New Orleans. St. Roch Tavern is the neighborhood's dive bar: local bands, karaoke, pinball, a pool table, strong pours, and a kitchen that does vegan food better than it needs to.

4.6★ from 359 Google reviews · 1200 St Roch Ave, New Orleans, LA 70117

Directions  @st.rochmarket on Instagram

St. Roch Tavern has anchored the corner of St. Roch and Marais for decades and has stayed exactly what the neighborhood wants: a no-frills bar with a stage in the corner, pinball along the wall, a pool table, a jukebox, and a crowd that's half regulars and half people about to become regulars.

Live music most weekends, karaoke on its night, and a small kitchen with real vegan options that surprises people. Cash only. Cheap. Local hub is the phrase the reviews use.

What to order at St Roch Tavern

  • Live Music: Local bands weekends, karaoke midweek. Check the chalkboard.
  • Pinball: A row of machines and people who take them seriously.
  • The Kitchen: Bar food with vegan options that people come for on purpose.
  • Strong Pours: Cash only, and the drinks make sure you notice.

Local tip

Karaoke night is the neighborhood at its most honest. Bring cash and a song.
