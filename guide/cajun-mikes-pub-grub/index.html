Cajun Mikes Pub & Grub

Pub in Central Business District, New Orleans. Cold beer, a jukebox, a gator on the sign, and a kitchen that takes the sandwich seriously. Since 2000.

4.3★ from 1,091 Google reviews · 116 Baronne St, New Orleans, LA 70112

Directions  @cajun_beignet on Instagram

Cajun Mike's has been on Baronne Street since 2000, which in this part of the CBD makes it an elder. It is a bar first: sixteen-ish stools, a long wood top, TVs for the game, and bartenders who refill before you ask. Regulars call it a hole in the wall and mean it as the highest compliment.

The kitchen is the surprise. The hot roast beef po'boy comes out dripping the way it should, the cochon de lait is slow roasted pork piled on Leidenheimer bread, and the gumbo is the real dark-roux kind. It is the spot locals send visitors when they want the sandwich without the Bourbon Street markup.

What to order at Cajun Mikes Pub & Grub

  • Hot Roast Beef Po'boy: Dressed, gravy soaking through the bread. Ask for extra spicy mustard.
  • Cochon de Lait: Slow roasted pork, on a po'boy or over fries when you cannot decide.
  • Boudin Balls: Fried crisp with remoulade. The right thing to order with the first beer.
  • Gator Sausage Sandwich: Alligator sausage, grilled onions, hot sauce. Tourists hesitate, then order a second.

Local tip

Two blocks from the Roosevelt and the Saenger. Perfect pre-show, and it is where the bartenders from the fancy places drink after.
