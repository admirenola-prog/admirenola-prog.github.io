Boondock Saint

Bar in French Quarter, New Orleans. Boondock Saint is the small, dark, friendly pub a block from Jackson Square. Guinness on tap, a good jukebox, no kitchen, and bartenders who will send you to the right next bar.

4.7★ from 379 Google reviews · 731 St Peter, New Orleans, LA 70116

Directions  @popular on Instagram

Named for the cult movie and run like a neighborhood pub, Boondock Saint is the French Quarter bar locals keep for themselves. It is small. It is dark in the good way. The Guinness pours right, the jukebox has taste, and the bartenders, Lacey among them, are the reason half the reviews exist.

No kitchen, so bring in a slice from across the street. Quiet on weekday afternoons, packed on the weekends, always a stool eventually. People-watch from the door and watch St. Peter Street go by.

What to order at Boondock Saint

  • The Guinness: Poured properly, a block from Jackson Square. Rarer than you'd think.
  • The Jukebox: Rock, punk, Irish, and whatever the bartender is in the mood for.
  • The Bartenders: Named in the reviews. They'll map out your night.
  • Iced Irish Coffee: Jameson, local cold brew, whole milk, Irish cream, whipped cream. Eight dollars. Perk up and chill out.

Local tip

Weekday afternoon is the locals' hour. Weekend late is the party. Both are worth it for different reasons.
