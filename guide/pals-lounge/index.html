Pal's Lounge

Bar in Bayou St. John, New Orleans. Pal's is the corner bar every neighborhood wishes it had. Creative cocktails, cheap beer, a patio, rotating food pop-ups, and a crowd that runs from twenty-two to seventy-two.

4.6★ from 707 Google reviews · 949 N Rendon, New Orleans, LA 70119

Directions

Pal's has been the bar on the corner of Rendon and St. Philip long enough that people give directions by it. It is a dive in the way New Orleans means the word: good lighting, a jukebox somebody curated, bartenders who move fast and remember your drink, dogs on the patio, and prices that let you stay for the second round.

The cocktail list is better than it needs to be, with names you'll want to read out loud. The beer is cold and local. The food changes with whoever is cooking in back that night, from Thai pop-ups to crawfish boils in season. Reviewers keep landing on the same two words: friendly and eclectic.

What to order at Pal's Lounge

  • The Cocktail List: House creations with names like Dennis Hopper on a Jet Ski. Two-person punches for $25.
  • Local Beer: Urban South, NOLA Brewing, Parish, plus the tallboy of the week for pocket change.
  • Pop-Up Nights: Thai, tacos, crawfish, whoever is cooking. Follow Instagram for the week's schedule.
  • The Patio: Outside on Rendon with the neighborhood walking by. Dog friendly, cigarette friendly, everyone friendly.

Local tip

Tuesday is pop-up night and the patio fills by eight. Walk over from the Bayou St. John end and you'll never fight for parking.
