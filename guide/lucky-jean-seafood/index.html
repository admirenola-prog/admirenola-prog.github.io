Lucky Jean Seafood

Seafood restaurant in New Orleans East Area, New Orleans. Fried shrimp by the pound, boiled crawfish in season, and a shrimp po'boy that needs two hands. Takeout, lunch and dinner, weekdays.

4.2★ from 805 Google reviews · 6746 Chef Menteur Hwy, New Orleans, LA 70126

Directions  @willajeanneworleans on Instagram

Lucky Jean's is the kind of place you find by asking someone from the East where they get their seafood. The answer comes fast. The little building on Chef Menteur Highway with the shrimp boat on the sign has been frying, boiling, and stuffing po'boys for the neighborhood for decades, and the regulars who have been coming since before the storm are still coming.

It is a counter, not a dining room. You order, you wait a few minutes, you leave with a box that smells like Sunday. Shrimp, fish, oysters when they have them, wings, gumbo, red beans on Monday, meat pies and egg rolls for the ride home.

What to order at Lucky Jean Seafood

  • Fried Shrimp: Light batter, Gulf shrimp, by the plate or the pound. The reason for the line.
  • Shrimp Po'boy: Overstuffed on French bread, dressed. Comes with a side you did not ask for and will eat.
  • Boiled Crawfish: In season, by the sack. Call ahead on Fridays.
  • Lemon Pepper Wings: The non-seafood order that regulars swear by.

Local tip

Call your order in. The lunch line is the neighborhood's, and it moves faster than it looks.
